# Bharat Renewable Energy - CMS Admin Guide

## 🎉 Welcome to Your Content Management System!

This CMS allows you to easily manage all content on your Bharat Renewable Energy website without touching any code.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Access the Admin Panel

1. Navigate to `/admin/login` in your browser
2. If this is your first time, click "Don't have an account? Sign up"
3. Create your admin account with:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (minimum 6 characters recommended)

### Step 2: Initialize the CMS

After logging in for the first time:
1. Click the **"Initialize CMS"** button in the top-right corner
2. This will populate your database with the current website content
3. You can now start editing!

---

## 📝 Managing Content

### Pages Management

**What you can edit:**
- Hero Section Title
- Hero Section Subtitle
- Hero Background Images
- Section Titles
- Page-specific content

**How to edit a page:**
1. Click on **"Pages"** in the sidebar
2. Find the page you want to edit
3. Click **"Edit"**
4. Make your changes in the text fields
5. Click **"Save"**
6. Changes are live immediately!

**Available Pages:**
- Home
- About
- Services (main content)
- Projects (main content)
- Safety (main content)
- Contact (main content)

---

### Services Management

**What you can do:**
- ✅ Add new services
- ✏️ Edit existing services
- 🗑️ Delete services

**Adding a new service:**
1. Click on **"Services"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Add Service"** button
3. Fill in:
   - Title (e.g., "Solar Panel Installation")
   - Description (what the service offers)
   - Image URL (paste image link)
4. Click **"Save"**

**Editing a service:**
1. Find the service in the list
2. Click **"Edit"**
3. Make your changes
4. Click **"Save"**

**Deleting a service:**
1. Find the service in the list
2. Click the trash icon
3. Confirm deletion

---

### Projects Management

**What you can do:**
- ✅ Add new projects to showcase
- ✏️ Edit existing projects
- 🗑️ Delete projects

**Adding a new project:**
1. Click on **"Projects"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Add Project"** button
3. Fill in:
   - Title (e.g., "5 MW Ground-Mount Solar Installation")
   - Client (e.g., "ABC Corporation")
   - Location (e.g., "Chennai, Tamil Nadu")
   - Scope (what was done)
   - Result (outcome/achievement)
   - Image URL (project photo)
4. Click **"Save"**

**Editing a project:**
1. Find the project in the list
2. Click **"Edit"**
3. Update the fields
4. Click **"Save"**

## 🖼️ Working with Images

### Using Image URLs

For now, images are managed using URLs. You can use:

1. **Unsplash** (free stock photos)
   - Go to unsplash.com
   - Find your image
   - Right-click → Copy image address
   - Paste into the Image URL field

2. **Your own hosted images**
   - Upload to any image hosting service
   - Copy the direct image link
   - Paste into the Image URL field

3. **Existing website images**
   - Check the current website
   - Copy image URLs from existing content

**Image Best Practices:**
- Use high-quality images (at least 1200px wide)
- Ensure images are relevant to the content
- Use consistent image styles across the site
- Compress images for faster loading

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

### Content Writing Tips

1. **Keep titles concise** - 8-12 words max
2. **Use clear descriptions** - Explain benefits, not just features
3. **Include specific numbers** - "50+ projects" is better than "many projects"
4. **Write for your audience** - Use language your customers understand
5. **SEO-friendly** - Include relevant keywords naturally

### Before Publishing Changes

- ✅ Preview your changes
- ✅ Check for typos
- ✅ Ensure images load correctly
- ✅ Test on mobile (if possible)
- ✅ Verify links work

### Regular Maintenance

**Weekly:**
- Review and update project showcase
- Add new testimonials
- Update any pricing or offers

**Monthly:**
- Check all images still load
- Review service descriptions
- Update company statistics

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "Unauthorized" Error
**Solution:** Log out and log back in. Your session may have expired.

### Content Not Saving
**Solutions:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Refresh the page and try again
3. Log out and log back in
4. Contact your developer if issue persists

### Images Not Showing
**Solutions:**
1. Verify the image URL is correct
2. Make sure the URL ends with an image extension (.jpg, .png, etc.)
3. Test the URL in a new browser tab
4. Use a different image hosting service

### Can't See New Content on Website
**Solutions:**
1. Hard refresh the page (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Try opening in an incognito/private window
